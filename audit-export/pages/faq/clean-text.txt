Frequently Asked Questions
Answers to common questions about Good Hemp Distro.
What is Good Hemp Distro?
Good Hemp Distro is a community marketplace connecting hemp producers, vendors, and buyers across the supply chain.
How do I become a vendor?
Register via Vendor Registration and complete the verification process. Once approved, you can list products immediately.
What products are listed on the marketplace?
We support CBD wellness products, industrial hemp, hemp-based services, and recreational hemp where legally permitted.
How does wholesale access work?
Wholesale buyers can apply through the Wholesale section. Access is granted after identity and business verification.
Who do I contact for support?
Reach us through the Contact or Support pages. Our team typically responds within one business day.
Still have questions?
Our support team is happy to help.
